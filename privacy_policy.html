Privacy Policy

AsantePost: Heritage & Culture

Effective Date: March 26, 2026 · Last Updated: March 26, 2026

MPG Smart Solutions ("we," "our," or "us") operates the AsantePost mobile application and web platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

By using AsantePost, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Biography (optional)

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from your Google account.

1.2 Device Information

We automatically collect:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system and version
  • App version
  • A unique device identifier

1.3 Location Data

With your permission, we may collect your approximate or precise location to:

  • Enhance community report features
  • Provide location-relevant cultural content

You can disable location access at any time through your device settings. The app will continue to function without location access.

1.4 Payment Information

When you make a purchase or donation, our payment processor (Paystack) handles the transaction. We collect:

  • Transaction reference and amount
  • Payment method type (Mobile Money, Card, or Bank Transfer)
  • Transaction status and timestamps

We do not store your card numbers, Mobile Money PINs, or banking credentials. All payment processing is handled securely by Paystack.

1.5 User-Generated Content

When you use the app's community features, we collect:

  • Forum posts and comments
  • Community reports (including attached photos)
  • Story submissions

1.6 Usage Data

We collect analytics data to improve the app experience:

  • Screens visited and features used
  • Search queries
  • Content engagement metrics
  • App performance data
  • Crash reports and error logs

You can disable analytics and crash reporting in the app's Privacy Settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the AsantePost app
  • Process transactions and send related information
  • Send push notifications about cultural events, Akwasidae dates, and new content (with your permission)
  • Monitor and analyze usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security threats
  • Moderate community content and enforce community guidelines
  • Respond to your support requests and inquiries

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers. We implement appropriate security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Firebase App Check for API security
  • Secure credential storage on your device
  • Server-side payment processing (no sensitive payment data on client)
  • Input validation and content moderation
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Update your profile information at any time

  • Revoke permissions (camera, location, calendar) through your device settings

To exercise your data rights, contact us at the email provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

AsantePost is intended for a general audience interested in Asante cultural heritage. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete such information.
