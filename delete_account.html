How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your AsantePost account and associated data using any of the methods below.

Method 1: In-App (Recommended)

  1. Open the AsantePost app on your device.
  2. Go to your Profile screen (tap your avatar in the top bar).
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your identity when prompted.
  6. Tap Permanently Delete to confirm.

Method 2: Email Request

If you cannot access the app, send an email to one of the addresses below with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Include the email address associated with your account.

  • samach25@yahoo.com
  • nooku2x@gmail.com

We will process your request within 7 business days and send a confirmation email once complete.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please make sure you want to proceed before confirming.

What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

Data Type Action Timeframe
Account credentials (email, password) Deleted Immediately
Profile information (name, photo) Deleted Immediately
Subscription & payment records Deleted Immediately
Saved preferences & settings Deleted Immediately
Notification tokens Deleted Immediately
Forum posts & comments Anonymized Within 30 days
Aggregated analytics data Retained (anonymous) N/A

Why is some data retained?

  • Forum posts & comments are anonymized (author name replaced with "Deleted User") rather than removed, to preserve the integrity of community discussions. The content remains but is no longer linked to your identity.
  • Aggregated analytics are fully anonymous and cannot be traced back to any individual user.

Additional Information

  • If you have an active subscription, please cancel it through the Google Play Store before deleting your account to avoid further charges.
  • Deletion removes your data from our primary systems. Backup copies may take up to 30 days to be fully purged.
  • This action does not delete data held by Google (e.g., Play Store purchase history). Please refer to Google's data management for those records.
